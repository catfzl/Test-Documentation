A Final Test Summary Report is a comprehensive document that provides a detailed overview of the testing activities conducted throughout the SDLC, summarizing the results and outcomes of the testing phase. It serves as a record of testing efforts, highlighting the scope of tests executed, the defects found, their resolution, and the overall quality of the product.

This document aims to demonstrate what the ideal format for a Test Plan should look like for IAM and IAM-related features.

# What Needs a Final Test Summary Report
A Final Test Summary Report is required for any feature, system, or product that has undergone formal testing and is being prepared for release or deployment. It is especially important in the following cases:

* ***Major Releases or Updates:*** When releasing new products, features, or major updates, the report provides a comprehensive record of the testing process and results.
* ***Critical Systems:*** For products that support critical business functions or sensitive data, the report ensures that all necessary testing has been completed and validated.
* ***Regulatory Compliance:*** When a product is subject to regulatory or compliance standards, the summary report documents the validation efforts and confirms adherence to those requirements.
* ***Cross-Team Testing:*** When multiple teams are involved in the testing process, the Final Test Summary Report synthesizes their efforts, making it easier for stakeholders to see the full picture.
* ***Risk Mitigation:*** In situations where a product has high risk, the report helps document any issues discovered during testing, their resolution, and any residual risks that need attention post-release.

# When to Complete a Final Test Summary Report
The Final Test Summary Report should be completed at the end of the testing phase, just before the product is deployed or released. 

This is typically after all planned tests have been executed, defects have been addressed, and the product is deemed ready for release. Ideally, it should be finalized in the *Testing (Verification and Validation) phase* but may be updated during the *Deployment phase* if additional testing activities or smoke tests are required.

* *Testing Phase:*
After all test cases have been executed, defects have been resolved, and the product has passed verification, the report should be finalized. It summarizes the testing efforts, captures any outstanding issues, and confirms the overall quality of the product.

* *Deployment Phase:*
If additional testing (e.g., smoke tests or post-deployment checks) is performed, the report may be updated to include final validations, confirming that the product functions as expected in the production environment.

The Final Test Summary Report should be delivered to key stakeholders, including Product Owners, the Engineering Manager, and other relevant team members, to provide transparency into the testing process and its outcomes before the product is officially released.
