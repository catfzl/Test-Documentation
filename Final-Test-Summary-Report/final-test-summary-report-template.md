# 2. Testing Scope

Testing activities covered the following areas:

* **Functional Testing:** Verification of core functional requirements, user workflows, and business rules.
* **Non-Functional Testing:** Includes performance, security, compatibility, and usability testing.
* **System Integration Testing (SIT):** Individual components or modules are combined and tested as a group to verify that they work together as expected within the larger system or application.
* **User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** Formal testing with respect to user needs, requirements, and business processes conducted to determine whether or not a system satisfies the acceptance criteria and to enable the user, customers, or other authorized entity to determine whether or not to accept the system.
